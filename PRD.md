PRD - Story-Creator-App-with-Voice-Recording-and-Sharing

The focus of this website and app is to allow children to generate stories by choosing the characters in the story. Children can choose the age of the child, how many characters, the names of the characters and the location, as well as the theme of the adventure. 

Pro users are allowed up to 5 characters and longer stories with images. Normal users only 2 characters and images. These characters and images are carried into the story each time

The app then takes all the children inputs and gives the child an option of 1 paragraph intro or 2. Just to get the child’s imagination going. The app will then ask the child to continue the story, or if they don’t want to continue then they can click “Robyn Reads” button to continue the story for another paragraph. The intro should be in one colour text, and any user input text will always be a different colour. E.g App text = black. User input text = Purple.

Once the story is completed the app creates a finished book with the users name as the author and illustrator and saved on the users profile. The app should also create a front cover and back cover with story synopsis.

Normal users save 3 books and stories, pro Users save 10+.

Pro users can send the book to be printed by Amazon KDP and sent to the saved address. 


The website will only create stories that are age appropriate for the child’s age. This is confirmed when starting the story. Max age rating is 13.
